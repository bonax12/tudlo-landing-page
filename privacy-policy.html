2. What personal information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry / trial-lesson form: your name, email address, who the lesson is for (child, teen, university student or adult), the skills you want to improve, and the free-text “goal” you choose to add.
  • Correspondence: anything you send us by email, or through WhatsApp or Telegram if you choose to contact us there, including scheduling details and information about the learner that you decide to share.
  • Booking & lesson records: once you book, a record of lessons scheduled and delivered, notes on learning goals and progress, and payment references (we do not store full card numbers — see “Payments”).

Information collected automatically

  • Hosting logs: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub keeps standard server logs, including your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to serve the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts: the site currently loads fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser contacts Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address. We are moving to self-hosted fonts to stop this; until then, you can block third-party requests in your browser.
  • Spam protection: our form provider (see below) processes your IP address and a hidden “honeypot” field to filter out automated submissions.
  • Cookies: we do not use analytics or advertising cookies. See our Cookies Policy.

We do not deliberately collect “special category” data (such as health, religion or ethnicity). Please don’t put any in the free-text goal field.

4. Children and young learners

Tudlo teaches children and teenagers, but our website and booking process are meant for adults. If the learner is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must make the enquiry and the booking, and gives consent on the child’s behalf.

  • EU/EEA: where we rely on consent for an online service offered to a child, the child must be at least 16 (or the lower age their country sets, between 13 and 16). Below that, the holder of parental responsibility must consent. In practice we rely on parent/guardian consent for every under-18 booking.
  • UK: the equivalent age is 13.
  • Republic of Korea: for a child under 14 we collect and use personal information only with a legal guardian’s consent, and we ask for the minimum information needed to obtain it.

If you think a child has given us personal information without the right consent, email support.tudlo@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights over your personal information:

  • Ask for a copy of it, and for mistakes to be corrected.
  • Ask us to delete it, or to pause or limit how we use it.
  • Ask us to send it to you or another provider in a portable form.
  • Object to processing we base on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already done.
  • (Korea) Ask us to suspend the processing of your personal information.

To use any of these, email support.tudlo@gmail.com. We respond within one month. There’s no fee unless the request is excessive or repetitive.

You can also complain to a regulator:

  • EU/EEA: your national data protection authority.
  • UK: the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
  • Korea: the Personal Information Protection Commission (pipc.go.kr), or KISA’s privacy call centre (dial 118).

We’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

9. Payments

If and when Tudlo takes payment, card and bank details are entered on our payment provider’s secure page and handled by them. Tudlo receives only a confirmation and a transaction reference — not your full card number.

10. How we protect your information

We keep access to your information limited to people who need it, use reputable providers that encrypt data in transit, protect accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication where available, and delete information when we no longer need it. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

11. Other websites

Our site and emails link to other services (for example a calendar booking page, WhatsApp or Telegram). This policy doesn’t cover them — read their own privacy policies before you use them.
