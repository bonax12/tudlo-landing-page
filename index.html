Frequently askedquestions

Not sure which path fits? We'll help you find the one that does.

How do I choose the right program?

Start with a free consultation. We'll consider the learner's age, comfort with English, goals, and schedule before recommending a welcoming next step.

Can parents book classes for their child?

Absolutely. Parents and guardians can book, share learning goals, and receive guidance on the best experience for their child.

Do you offer lessons for teens and adults too?

Yes. Tudlo has age-right programs for teen learners, university students, and working adults—each built around practical goals.

Can I learn online?

Yes. Tudlo lessons are live and online, so you can join from home on a schedule that works for your routine.
