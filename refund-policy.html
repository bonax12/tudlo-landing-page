1. Scope

This policy applies to consultations, trial lessons, single lessons and lesson packages booked through Tudlo. Words defined in the Terms & Conditions have the same meaning here.

2. Free consultation

The consultation is free of charge, so there is nothing to refund.

3. Trial lesson

[If the trial is free: there is nothing to refund.] [If the trial is paid: the fee is refundable if you cancel at least [24 hours] before the scheduled start. Once the trial lesson has been delivered, the fee is not refundable.]

4. Your 14-day right to cancel (consumers in the EEA and the UK)

If you are a consumer resident in the EEA or the UK, you may cancel your booking within 14 days of our booking confirmation, for any reason, by emailing support.tudlo@gmail.com. You can use wording like:

“I give notice that I withdraw from my contract for the following lessons: [your name, booking reference, lesson dates].”

  • If no lessons have been delivered, we refund you in full within [14 days].
  • If you asked for lessons to begin within the 14-day period, you may still cancel, but we may keep a proportionate amount for the lessons (and any consultation time) already delivered up to the moment you tell us to stop, and we refund the balance.
  • Once every lesson in the booking has been delivered within the 14 days, this right no longer applies.

This right is in addition to everything below. Nothing in this policy removes it.

5. Lesson packages — unused lessons

You can stop a package at any time. We refund lessons you have paid for but not yet taken, [minus a [X]% administration fee] and [minus the difference between the discounted package rate and the standard single-lesson rate for the lessons you have already taken].

Unused lessons expire [X months] after purchase and are not refundable after that date.

6. Rescheduling and missed lessons

  • Reschedule free of charge with at least [24 hours] notice.
  • If you cancel or reschedule with less than [24 hours] notice, or don’t attend, the lesson is treated as delivered and is not refunded or credited. [Optional goodwill: one late reschedule per package is allowed.]

7. If Tudlo cancels

  • If we or the tutor cancel a lesson, or a lesson can’t go ahead because of a problem on our side, you choose a free reschedule or a full refund of that lesson.
  • If we stop providing a package partway through, we refund every undelivered lesson in full.

8. Illness and emergencies

[Optional: with reasonable notice, and evidence where relevant, we will waive the late-cancellation rule in section 6 for genuine illness or emergencies, at our discretion.]

9. How refunds are paid

Refunds are made to your original payment method within [14 days] of us agreeing the refund. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show it. We do not refund third-party transfer charges or currency-conversion fees.

10. How to request a refund

Email support.tudlo@gmail.com with your name, booking reference and the reason for the request. We reply within [5 working days].

11. If we can’t agree

Please raise any complaint with us first at support.tudlo@gmail.com. Consumers in the EU can also use the European Commission’s Online Dispute Resolution platform at ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr. You keep the right to take the matter to the courts or a consumer body in your country of residence.

12. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy for future bookings. The version that applies to your booking is the one published when you booked.
